ABOUT NEXHIRE-AI

Your Personal Interview Preparation Partner

NexHire-AI is an AI-powered mock interview platform designed to help students, freshers, and job seekers improve their interview preparation.

Users can select a company and job role, participate in a simulated interview, submit their answers, and receive results with personalized feedback.

The platform also maintains interview history so users can track their preparation and performance over time.
